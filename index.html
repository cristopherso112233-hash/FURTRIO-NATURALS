A LITTLE ABOUT US

Made for the ones who make a house a home.

Furtio Naturals is for the muddy-pawed greeters, sunny-window nappers, curious whiskers, and the people who love them. We make little-batch bath bars with one simple thought in mind: bath time can be a gentler, more thoughtful part of caring for your furry family.

Small batches, made with care

Each bar is made in little batches, with a hands-on approach from our studio to your shelf.

Ingredients up front

We want you to know what's in every bar, so you can make an informed choice for your pet.

For paws and whiskers

From goat milk and honey soap to a simple wash-day routine, our goal is to make everyday care feel a little more considered.

A gentle, nourishing wash

Give your furry friend gentle, natural care with Furtrio Naturals Goat Milk & Honey Soap! 🐾

Crafted with premium, skin-loving ingredients — perfect for pets with sensitive skin. Rich in lactic acid from fresh goat milk to gently exfoliate, while honey and nourishing oils soothe and moisturize their coat. Safe, mild, and made especially for your fur babies! 💛
